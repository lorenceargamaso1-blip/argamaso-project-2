About our school

A legacy of faith and education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has been part of the educational life of Kabankalan City for generations. KCC seeks to form learners who grow in knowledge, character, faith, and a commitment to serve others.

Guided by the call “Teach Ye All Nations” (Matthew 28:19), the college brings Catholic identity into learning, community engagement, and everyday student life.
